## CORE CONCEPT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
A modern herbal garden series inspired by 6 iconic Vietnamese 
herbal/wellness destinations. Each app screen draws from a 
specific real Vietnamese location, giving the app cultural 
authenticity + PR value.

contemporary design.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
## VIETNAMESE LOCATION MAPPING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
Each screen maps to a real Vietnamese herbal heritage site:

| Screen | Location | Why this place |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Garden Home** | **Tam Đảo (Vĩnh Phúc)** | National herbal medicine garden, foggy hills, cool climate, the "spiritual home" of Vietnamese pharmacology |
| **Family Garden** | **Mộc Châu (Sơn La)** | Plateau tea fields, terraced layers visible from above, family farming culture |
| **Care Plan / Detail** | **Sapa (Lào Cai)** | Atisô fields, healing herbal traditions of Hmong people, terraced beauty |
| **Voucher Shop** | **Hội An (Quảng Nam)** | Lantern-lit ancient markets, gift-giving culture, warm sunset |
| **Loading / Meditation** | **Yên Tử (Quảng Ninh)** | Sacred herbal mountain (Trúc Lâm Buddhism), but use peaceful pine forest aspect (NOT religious imagery) |
| **Profile / Memorial** | **Bắc Hà (Lào Cai)** | Sâm Ngọc Linh region, herb harvesting heritage |

**Rule:** Never name the location in the artwork itself. The 
location is the *inspiration*, captured through subtle visual 
cues (terraced fields = Sapa, foggy pines = Tam Đảo, etc.).

## SCENE BUILDING — MODERN HILLSIDE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

DO include:

**Modern Vietnamese architecture:**
- Clean wooden veranda with minimalist tile roof corners 
  (curved tiles but simplified, less ornate than traditional)
- Concrete + wood hybrid structures (Vo Trong Nghia style)
- Bamboo screens used as modern design element
- Glass panels integrated with wood frames
- Stone pathway with clean lines (not random rocks)
- Modern raised planter beds in wood or concrete

**Vietnamese landscape elements:**
- Terraced fields (ruộng bậc thang) in middle distance
- Pine forest or bamboo grove in background
- Soft morning mist rolling between hills (NEVER covering sky)
- Distant mountain silhouettes (atmospheric perspective)
- Tea plant rows visible (for Mộc Châu scenes)
- Atisô plants (for Sapa scenes)

**Modern wellness details:**
- Glass dome over precious herbs (greenhouse element)
- Modern stainless watering can (clean lines, not rustic)
- Wood and brass accents on garden tools
- Stone planters with crisp edges
- Subtle natural lighting (no harsh contrast)

**Lifestyle touches:**
- A modern wooden bench or lounge chair (1 element max)
- Hanging Hội An lanterns (for warmth)
- Small modern pendant lamps (for evening scenes)
- Hot tea cup on wooden surface (cozy detail)

DON'T include:

❌ "Trên mây" framing (no sky islands, no floating)
❌ Cõi tiên / divine imagery (no halos, no celestial)
❌ Death-adjacent symbols (white doves, lotus on water, candles)
❌ Religious iconography (no Buddha, no temples even Yên Tử)
❌ Rustic clutter (no piles of random pottery, hanging junk)
❌ Ethnic minority costumes on people (cultural sensitivity)
❌ Snow or extreme cold (not Vietnamese climate)
❌ Western pastoral elements (no European cottages)
❌ Anime sharp features
❌ Modern technology visible (no phones, screens, cables)
❌ Cars, motorbikes, urban infrastructure

## PLANT STYLE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
Vietnamese highland medicinal plants in modern presentation:
- Recognizable real herbs (Bạc Hà, Gừng, Nghệ, Sen, Tía Tô, 
  Cam Thảo, Atisô, etc.)
- Botanical accuracy with stylized softness
- Pots: Modern terracotta with clean simple shapes OR 
  Bát Tràng ceramic with subtle modern patterns
- 5 growth stages clearly distinguishable
- Mature plants (Lv.5) have soft golden glow at leaf edges
- NO heavy magical sparkles — keep modern restraint

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
## LIGHTING DIRECTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════
- Primary source: Morning sun from east (upper-left preferred)
- Color temperature: Warm but not orange-saturated golden hour
- Quality: Soft diffused through morning mist
- Volumetric god rays through pine trees or terraced fields
- Soft long shadows (not harsh)
- Atmospheric haze in distance (depth perception)
- Some scenes: late afternoon golden hour (Hội An scenes)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════
